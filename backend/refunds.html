Refund Policy — Guild Hall

Last updated: 10/08/2026

In short: try Guild Hall free for 7 days. If you're not happy after you've paid, ask for a refund within 14 days of the charge and you get your money back. No reason needed.

1. Who handles payments

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle is the Merchant of Record for all our orders, so Paddle processes refunds. This policy sits alongside Paddle's Refund Policy and Paddle's Buyer Terms.

2. Free trial

A new Guild Hall subscription starts with a 7-day free trial. You need a payment method to start it, but nothing is charged during the trial. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

3. 14-day money-back guarantee

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of any payment. This covers the first payment after the trial and every monthly renewal. You don't have to give a reason.

4. How to ask for a refund

  • Go to paddle.net, look up your order with the email you used at checkout, and request a refund; or
  • reply to the receipt email Paddle sent you; or
  • message us (see Contact below), and we'll ask Paddle to issue it.

The money goes back to the payment method you paid with. When it shows up depends on your bank or card provider.

5. Refunds and your subscription

A refund doesn't stop future renewals by itself. To stop being charged, cancel from Manage billing in Guild Settings, or ask for the cancellation when you request the refund. Once a subscription ends, the guild follows the lapse rules in our Terms of Service: 7 days of grace, then the hall is suspended. Your guild's data is kept while the hall is suspended.

6. Cancelling without a refund

You can cancel at any time from Manage billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and the guild keeps full access until then. After the 14-day window, we don't refund the part of a period you didn't use. Paddle may still grant a refund at its discretion.

7. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the consumer law where you live.
