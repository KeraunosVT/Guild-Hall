Discord bot

/command reference

Five commands — eight forms once you count the LOA variants — run from any channel in your guild's Discord server. Times are read in your guild's own timezone, and everything you file lands in your guild's records only: the bot serves several houses and never mixes them. One thing the bot does isn't a command at all: signup posts are answered with buttons.

Anyone in the guild — 6 forms

/elitetimer Everyone

Records that a world boss died and works out when it comes back. Your confirmation is private; the timer itself is posted to the channel so everyone sees it.

/elitetimer location:Laslan time:6:40pm
OptionNotes
locationrequiredPick from the list — Laslan, Stoneguard, Talandre, their Abyss variants, and Nyx.
timerequiredWhen it died, today, in guild time. 6:40pm, 640pm and 1840 all work.
Respawn Laslan and Stoneguard return after 4 hours; Talandre and Nyx after 6. Abyss variants match their surface counterpart. Reporting the same location twice replaces the earlier timer rather than adding a second.
/elitetimers Everyone

Lists every boss with its next spawn as a live countdown. No options. The reply is public, so it doubles as a way to put the current state in front of the whole channel.

/loa event Everyone

Miss one night. Name a specific event, or leave the event blank and give a start time to mean "out from then on" — useful when you'll be late rather than absent.

/loa event date:2026-08-14 reason:work event:Siege start_time:9:00 PM
OptionNotes
daterequiredThe night you're out, as YYYY-MM-DD.
reasonrequiredSeen by officers only, never by other members.
eventoptionalStarts suggesting as you type. Leave blank to mean the whole night.
start_timeoptionalAbsent from this time onward. Blank means just the event you picked.
end_timeoptionalBack after this. Blank means out for the rest of the night.
memberofficersFile on someone else's behalf. Refused if you aren't an officer.
/loa range Everyone

A holiday, a work trip, anything spanning several days. Both ends are inclusive.

/loa range start_date:2026-08-14 end_date:2026-08-21 reason:holiday
OptionNotes
start_daterequiredFirst night out, YYYY-MM-DD.
end_daterequiredLast night out, inclusive.
reasonrequiredOfficers only.
memberofficersFile on someone else's behalf.
/loa recurring Everyone

A standing conflict — a class, a shift, a weekly commitment. Applies every week until you cancel it, so you file it once rather than every Tuesday.

/loa recurring day:Tuesday reason:class start_time:8:00 PM
OptionNotes
dayrequiredPick a weekday from the list.
reasonrequiredOfficers only.
eventoptionalBlank means the whole night, every week.
start_timeoptionalOut from this time onward.
end_timeoptionalBack after this.
memberofficersFile on someone else's behalf.
/loa cancel Everyone

Plans changed. Start typing and your upcoming LOAs appear — pick one and it's withdrawn, along with the notice that was posted when you filed it.

/loa cancel entry:Event — 2026-08-14
OptionNotes
entryrequiredSuggests your own upcoming LOAs. Officers see everyone's.
Scope Members can only cancel what they filed. Officers can cancel anyone's — the same rule the LOA board on the website follows.

Officers only — 2 forms

/attendance Officers

Takes a snapshot of who is sitting in a voice channel right now and records it as attendance for a scheduled event. Everyone captured gets a DM confirming it, so there's no argument later about whether they were counted.

/attendance event:Siege channel:#raid-1 date:2026-08-14
OptionNotes
eventrequiredWhich scheduled event this counts toward. Suggests as you type.
channeloptionalDefaults to the voice channel you're in. Name one to snap a different room.
dateoptionalDefaults to tonight. Use YYYY-MM-DD to backfill.
Timing It captures the moment you run it, not the whole night — run it once the group has actually assembled.
/announce Officers

Posts to the announcements channel with a Discord timestamp, so every member sees the time in their own zone and a live countdown beside it. No more "9:30 my time or yours".

/announce time:9:30pm message:roll call {time} in CTA comms role:@Members
OptionNotes
timerequiredToday, in guild time. 9:30pm, 930pm, 2130.
messageoptionalPut {time} where the timestamp should sit. Without it, the time is appended.
roleoptionalPings this role. Leave blank for a quiet post.

Signup posts — buttons, not commands

I'm in  ·  Withdraw  ·  Who's coming? Everyone

When officers open signups for a night, the bot posts the event with three buttons. There is nothing to type — press one, and the post updates itself with who is coming, grouped by Tank, DPS and Healer.

ButtonNotes
I'm incounts youPressing it twice changes nothing — you are counted once or not at all. If the night is capped and full, you join a waitlist instead and are told your place in it.
Withdrawundoes itTakes your name back off. If you were holding a slot on a capped night, whoever has waited longest moves into it straight away.
Who's coming?privateThe full list and the role split, answered only to you so the channel stays clean.
Not a no There is no "can't make it" button, and that is deliberate. Signing up says I'm coming; saying nothing means nobody knows either way. If you know you're out, file it with /loa — that is the record officers plan around, and withdrawing is not the same thing.
The ping Officers can have the post @-mention a role when it goes up. That happens once, when the announcement is first posted — the message is edited every time somebody signs up or drops out, and none of those edits notify anyone.
Nudges A night may carry a reminder some minutes before it starts. It is a DM, and it only goes to people who have neither pressed a button nor filed an LOA — never to anyone who has already answered either way. It is sent once, however many times an officer asks for it.
Old posts still work The buttons keep working after the bot restarts, so a post from last week is still live. They disappear entirely once the event's start time passes, rather than sitting there greyed out.

Things that apply everywhere

Replies are private

Everything answers only to you, except /elitetimers and the spawn timer posted after a kill report. Your LOA reason is never shown in channel.

Times are guild time

Every time you type is read in the guild's configured timezone, not your own and not the server's. Officers set that once.

The night rolls over after midnight

A 12:30 AM boss belongs to the night before, not to a new day. Schedules and LOA both follow that boundary, so late events stay attached to the right night.

Officer means your guild's role

Each guild names its own officer role. If a command answers Officers only, you don't hold it — ask whoever runs your roster.

New server? Give it an hour

Commands are registered across every server the bot serves, and Discord can take up to an hour to show them after it joins. Nothing is broken in the meantime.

Unregistered servers get nothing

If the bot is in a server that isn't set up in Guild Hall, every command politely refuses rather than writing to some other guild's records.
